## 终版高分范文

### 题目要求 (Directions)

Suppose you are organizing an online meeting. Write an e-mail to Jack, an international student, to
1) invite him to participate, and
2) tell him the details.
You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.
Do not use your own name. Use "Li Ming" instead. (10 points)

---

### 满分定制范文 (10/10 Model Essay)

Dear Jack,

    I hope this email finds you well. I am writing with immense pleasure to cordially invite you to participate in our upcoming virtual conference.
    The conference is scheduled to convene at 7:00 p.m. on Friday, October 15th, via Zoom. Revolving around cross-cultural exchange, this session aims to cement enduring bonds of mutual understanding between Chinese and foreign students. Given your multicultural background, your presence as a guest speaker would provide peers with invaluable insights.
    I would genuinely appreciate it if you could confirm your availability prior to this Wednesday. Please feel free to reach out to me should any questions arise.

Best regards,
Li Ming

---

### 结构与语言亮点复盘 (Pedagogical Review)

1. **视觉结构 (2-6-2 黄金律)**：
   - 首段（2 句，24 词）：同侪得体问候切入，开宗明义正式发出线上研讨会邀约；
   - 次段（3 句，50 词）：时空要素高密交代（`is scheduled to convene via Zoom`）+ 动词短语承重（`cement enduring bonds of mutual understanding`）+ 诚邀同位与虚拟语气；
   - 尾段（2 句，27 词）：现代同侪得体 RSVP 答复期盼 + 条件句倒装应急支持（`should any questions arise`），结语使用同侪得体的 `Best regards,`。
2. **全篇词数**：正文 101 词，严格受控于 90~110 词满分区间。
3. **生死红线合规**：零缩写、零感叹号、称呼英文逗号、署名无句号、首行缩进 4 字符且段间不空行、拒绝虚构会议ID细节。
