## 1. 建议信（Letter of Advice / Suggestion）

### 核心使命与语调契约
- **交际使命**：向权威机构、主办方或朋友提出切实可行的改进方案，提升质效；
- **语调要求**：委婉客气、建设性（Constructive）、避免命令语气，彻底淘汰生硬的 `should`。

### 三段式实战推进公式
- **首段（2 句速决）**：身份界定 + 开宗明义亮明建议议题
  `I am Li Ming, a [Identity]. I am writing this letter with the purpose of presenting some constructive recommendations regarding [Issue].`
  *(致同侪朋友时：`Hearing that you intend to [Goal], I am pleased to share some practical suggestions that may contribute to your plan.`)*
- **次段（3 句承重）**：过渡引出 + 措施一（明智之策+成效）+ 措施二（寄予期望+目的）
  `To begin with, it would be a sound policy for you to [Action 1], which will notably [Benefit 1]. Furthermore, you are kindly expected to [Action 2], so as to effectively [Benefit 2].`
- **尾段（2 句闭环）**：谦逊期盼 + 感谢与期待回复
  `I sincerely hope that these humble proposals will prove beneficial. Thank you for your kind consideration.`

## 2. 推荐信（Letter of Recommendation）

### 核心使命与语调契约
- **交际使命**：为朋友、留学生或机构推荐书籍、电影、景点或人才；
- **语调要求**：热情真挚（Enthusiastic）、理据充分，善用同位语融入文化底蕴与实用攻略 Tips 双拼。

### 三段式实战推进公式
- **首段（2 句速决）**：因情设辞寒暄 + 亮明推荐对象
  `I hope this letter finds you well. Learning that you are planning [Plan], I am writing with immense enthusiasm to recommend [Target] to you.`
- **次段（3 句承重）**：同位语定性特色 + 核心亮点深入描述 + 实用攻略 Tips 配套
  `[Target], an iconic [Category] boasting millennia of historical grandeur, serves as an ideal choice for you. Its compelling features will surely offer you an unforgettable experience. For your itinerary, it is strongly advisable to [Practical Tip] ahead of time.`
- **尾段（2 句闭环）**：确信体验美妙 + 提供后续咨询协助
  `I am confident that this journey will leave you with marvelous memories. Please feel free to reach out if you need any further assistance.`

## 3. 投诉信（Letter of Complaint）

### 核心使命与语调契约
- **交际使命**：针对产品瑕疵或服务不公向商家或机构交涉索赔；
- **语调要求**：先扬后抑、理智克制、坚决维权、提供具体订单事实，杜绝无理谩骂。

### 三段式实战推进公式
- **首段（2 句速决）**：克制引出投诉事实 + 交代时间订单信息
  `I feel compelled to write this letter to lodge a formal complaint regarding [Product/Service] that I purchased from your store on [Date] (Order No. [Number]).`
- **次段（3 句承重）**：肯定既往信誉 ➔ 揭示严重瑕疵 ➔ 客服怠慢 ➔ 索赔三件套诉求
  `I have long been a loyal customer of your brand owing to your reputation for reliability. Nevertheless, this gadget persistently suffers from [Flaw 1] and [Flaw 2]. Although I contacted your helpline, the staff seemed quite uncooperative. Under these circumstances, I feel fully justified in requesting an immediate replacement or a full refund.`
- **尾段（2 句闭环）**：敦促限期解决 + 保留法律维权途径
  `Your prompt attention and satisfactory resolution to this matter would be highly appreciated. I look forward to hearing from you soon.`

## 4. 道歉信（Letter of Apology）

### 核心使命与语调契约
- **交际使命**：因失约、迟到或损坏物品向对方表达真诚歉意，重塑人际信任；
- **语调要求**：客气诚挚、不可抗力归因、主动全额买单补救。

### 三段式实战推进公式
- **首段（2 句速决）**：诚挚致歉 + 点明未能履约的事由
  `I am writing this letter to convey my sincerest apologies to you for my failure to [Commitment].`
- **次段（3 句承重）**：原定安排 ➔ 突发不可抗力归因 ➔ 体谅对方失望 ➔ 实操买单补救方案
  `I had fully intended to [Original Plan]; regrettably, [Unforeseen Event], which rendered it impossible for me to arrive on schedule. You must be understandably disappointed by my sudden absence. To remedy my blunder, could we possibly rearrange the appointment at your convenience?`
- **尾段（2 句闭环）**：重申歉意 + 期盼宽恕与重新约定
  `Once again, please accept my heartfelt apologies for the inconvenience caused. I sincerely hope you can forgive my thoughtlessness.`

## 5. 邀请信（Letter of Invitation）

### 核心使命与双轨语调契约
- **交际使命**：邀请学者、专家或友人出席学术研讨会、文化节或庆典；
- **双轨语调与敬语契约**：
  - **A 轨·致学者专家/上位者（知道姓名）**：`Dear Professor Wang,` ➔ 庄重得体、谦逊尊崇，结语使用 `Yours sincerely,`；
  - **B 轨·致留学生同侪/朋友（私人书信）**：`Dear XXX,` ➔ 随和亲切、平等互动，结语使用 `Best wishes,` 或 `Kind regards,`（亦可用 `Best regards,`，严禁使用 `Yours sincerely,` 或 `favor us with a reply` 等老派官僚外交辞令）。

### 三段式实战推进公式
- **首段（2 句速决）**：
  - *官方学者邀请*：`On behalf of [Organization], I have the great honor of inviting you to be our keynote speaker at the upcoming [Event].`
  - *同侪活动邀请*：`As an organizer of our [Activity], I am writing to invite you to our online meeting scheduled for [Time] via [Platform].`
- **次段（3 句承重）**：精确时空要素 ➔ 受众规模与宗旨 ➔ 互动形式与演讲/讨论价值
  `The event is scheduled to convene at [Time] on [Date] in [Venue]. Given your unique background in this domain, your presence would provide participants with invaluable perspectives. Following the main session, an interactive Q&A discussion will be incorporated.`
- **尾段（2 句闭环）**：
  - *官方*：`We would be truly grateful if you could favor us with a reply confirming your availability by [Deadline].`
  - *同侪*：`We would be thrilled if you could join us. Looking forward to our lively discussion.`

## 6. 感谢信（Letter of Gratitude）

### 核心使命与语调契约
- **交际使命**：感谢导师、友人或房东在访学、求学或困难期间的关照；
- **语调与敬语契约**：
  - 致导师/房东（知道姓名）：`Dear Professor Smith,` ➔ 庄重谦逊，结语使用 `Yours sincerely,`；
  - 致友人/同侪（朋友私人书信）：`Dear XXX,` ➔ 深情真挚，结语使用 `Best wishes,` 或 `Kind regards,`（盛赞倾力付出、行动与物质双向回馈闭环）。

### 三段式实战推进公式
- **首段（2 句速决）**：直抒胸臆，致以最深谢意
  `I am writing this letter to express my deepest and most sincere gratitude for your selfless assistance and generous hospitality during [Occasion].`
- **次段（3 句承重）**：虚拟语气盛赞付出 ➔ 忆起具体感人细节 ➔ 寄送薄礼或经验反馈
  `Had it not been for your timely encouragement and wise counsel, I could never have adapted to the local environment so smoothly. I can still recall vividly how patiently you guided my studies. As a modest token of my appreciation, I have mailed you some exquisite gifts, which I hope you will enjoy.`
- **尾段（2 句闭环）**：发出回访邀请 + 祝愿健康顺遂
  `Whenever you visit Beijing in the future, please let me know so that I can return your wonderful kindness. Wish you health and happiness.`

## 7. 求职/自荐信（Letter of Application）

### 核心使命与语调契约
- **交际使命**：向高校、外企或公益机构申请助教、实习或志愿岗位；
- **语调要求**：严谨专业、自信得体、突出人岗匹配与硬核资质。

### 三段式实战推进公式
- **首段（2 句速决）**：信息源头 + 明确应聘岗位
  `Having learned from your official website that a [Position] is currently available, I am writing with keen interest to submit my formal application.`
- **次段（3 句承重）**：学业优势 ➔ 核心技能 ➔ 过往相关实践经验与团队奉献意识
  `Having maintained an outstanding academic record, I possess proficient bilingual fluency and solid pedagogical competencies. In addition, my previous experience as a peer tutor has endowed me with exceptional patience and interpersonal tact, enabling me to communicate effectively with students.`
- **尾段（2 句闭环）**：引荐随信简历 + 表达面试期盼
  `Enclosed with this letter is my detailed resume for your favorable review. I would appreciate the opportunity for an interview at your convenience.`

## 8. 告示 / 通知（Notice）

### 核心使命与语调契约
- **交际使命**：面向全校师生发布活动预告、人员招募或规则调整；
- **体例红线**：标题居中无称呼（Notice 或 Announcement），日期写在标题下一行的右侧，右下落款发布机构（严禁署名 Li Ming）。

### 三段式实战推进公式
- **首段（2 句速决）**：主办方 + 宗旨 + 正式宣布活动与招募名额
  `With the purpose of [Objective], the [Organization] is scheduled to launch [Event] on [Date]. We are presently recruiting [Number] dedicated student volunteers across campus.`
- **次段（3 句承重）**：岗位核心职责 ➔ 申请者硬核资质要求 ➔ 优选特长与行前培训
  `Qualified candidates are expected to demonstrate fluent spoken English alongside commendable communication skills. Furthermore, applicants with proven experience in digital photography or editing will enjoy definitive priority.`
- **尾段（2 句闭环）**：提交渠道 + 截止日期 + 热情动员
  `Those who are interested are warmly encouraged to submit their application forms to [Email] prior to [Deadline].`

## 9. 会议纪要（Meeting Minutes）

### 核心使命与语调契约
- **交际使命**：记录筹备会议或学术研讨的核心决议，供团队备忘与执行；
- **体例红线**：顶格五大要素头，客观被动语态，右下落款记录人，严控词数（≤120词）。

### 三段式实战推进公式
- **要素头**：
  `Date: [Date] | Place: [Place] | Present: [Attendees] | Absent: [Absentees] | Subject: [Subject]`
- **首段（1 句速决）**：交代开会时间、主持人与决议主旨
  `The meeting commenced at 3:00 p.m. with [Presiding Officer] presiding over the discussion. The core resolutions reached are summarized as follows.`
- **次段（3 句承重）**：分条客观陈述决议（场地 ➔ 宣传 ➔ 预算安全）
  `Firstly, regarding venue allocation, it was agreed that [Resolution 1]. Secondly, in terms of publicity, [Assignee] was assigned to [Task 2]. Lastly, the safety protocols and budget distribution were approved unanimously.`
- **落款**：右下角顶格注明 `Recorder: Li Ming`。

## 10. 交互式回信（Interactive Reply Letter）

### 核心使命与语调契约
- **交际使命**：针对对方来信提出的疑惑、焦虑或求助提供定制解答与建议；
- **语调要求**：共情回应（Empathic）、直奔主题、实操落地、同侪平等得体。

### 三段式实战推进公式
- **首段（2 句速决）**：情绪共情应答 + 欣然表示愿施以援手
  `I hope this email finds you well. In response to your understandable anxiety regarding [Task], I am writing to share several practical suggestions.`
- **次段（3 句承重）**：核心切入人物/举措 ➔ 分步骤具象展开 ➔ 视觉/多媒体辅助与排练
  `An effective starting point would be zeroing in on one iconic figure, such as [Figure], whose [Work] offers rich narrative material. Furthermore, you are expected to incorporate concise diagrams and slides to engage your audience. Lastly, conducting a timed rehearsal beforehand will significantly bolster your onstage confidence.`
- **尾段（2 句闭环）**：确信报告圆满成功 + 提供预演演练支持
  `I am confident that your presentation will turn out to be both informative and compelling. Feel free to let me know if you would like to practice with me beforehand. Best of luck.`
