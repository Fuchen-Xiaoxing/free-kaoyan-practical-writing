## 一、三大高频病灶与破局心法速查

| 典型病灶形态 | 典型病句反例 | 病理解析 | 方法论破局心法 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. 静态系表贫血症** | *The meeting is very important for students to communicate.* | 动词毫无推进力，全靠初级形容词 *important / good* 凑字数，缺乏物理动作。 | **提炼原子高能动作 (`do sth`)**：用具象行为动词（`enhance mutual communication`）替代静态 `is important`。 |
| **2. 悬空宽泛名词** | *We can do many things to solve this big problem.* | 名词极度笼统单薄（*things, problems, ways*），概念悬空。 | **名词三步构建法 (`sth`)**：精准范畴词 ➔ 前置定性形容词 ➔ 后置介词短语定格。 |
| **3. 动作流水账无闭环** | *We will hold a meeting. Jack will come. We will talk.* | 简单句碎裂堆叠，缺乏逻辑连接与因果推进机制。 | **动词前后扩充法**：前置契机 + 核心推进 + 后置效应（基础版用 `which will...`，高级版可用 `thereby doing...`）。 |

## 二、两阶段造句重构边界契约（严守物理隔离）

为确保基础版与高级版严格物理隔离，造句示范必须严格分阶：

| 教学阶段 | 允许使用的结构武器 | 禁用结构清单 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **阶段 1：基础版批改教学** | • 原子动宾结构 (`do sth`)<br>• 基础定语从句 (`which will + verb`)<br>• 基础目的状语 (`in order to / so as to`)<br>• 基础并列复合句 (`not only... but also...`) | ❌ **禁用伴随状语 (`thereby doing...`)**<br>❌ **禁用虚拟倒装 (`Had it not been for...`)**<br>❌ **禁用重度名词化与冷僻学术词** |
| **阶段 2：高级版升华教学** | • 分词伴随状语 (`thereby facilitating...`)<br>• 经典虚拟倒装 (`Had it not been for your guidance...`)<br>• 独立主格与介词短语前置<br>• 外刊级高阶动词承重与精准同位语 | ❌ **禁用无中生有编造细节**<br>❌ **禁用脱离大纲的 GRE 偏难怪词** |

## 三、原子信息模型：两大核心构件重构工具箱

英语表达的底层物理实质只有两个基本原子构件：**高能动词短语 (`do sth`)** 与 **高质名词短语 (`sth`)**。

### 构件 1：高质名词短语 (`sth`) 的“三步构建法”

```
[基准名词精准归位] ──> [前置修饰注入态度与格调] ──> [后置介词短语细节定格]
```

1. **第一步·基准名词精准归位**：
   - 拒用 *bad thing / problem* ➔ 选用精准范畴词：*dilemma, bottleneck, hurdle, obstacle, vulnerability*；
   - 拒用 *good way / idea* ➔ 选用精准范畴词：*initiative, approach, policy, remedy, strategy*；
   - 拒用 *difference* ➔ 选用精准范畴词：*discrepancy, divergence, gap*；
   - 拒用 *cooperation* ➔ 选用精准范畴词：*synergy, mutual understanding, consensus*。
2. **第二步·前置修饰注入态度与格调**：
   - 拒用 *good, bad, big*，选用具备学术与公文质感的定性形容词：
     - *pressing / acute / severe*（紧迫严峻的）；
     - *tailored / viable / pragmatic*（量身定制/切实可行的）；
     - *profound / substantial / invaluable*（深远/实质性/宝贵的）；
     - *erratic / substandard*（不稳定/不合规的）。
3. **第三步·后置介词短语细节定格**：
   - 用介词短语将宽泛名词牢牢锁定在特定时空、对象或领域：
     - `... across campus / in underdeveloped rural areas`（空间定格）；
     - `... between Chinese and foreign students`（群体定格）；
     - `... during final exam intervals`（时机定格）。

> **三步法实战装配案例**：
> - 初始想法：*communication*（交流，悬空单薄）
> - ① 选用基准词：`communication`
> - ② 注入前置定性：`mutual communication`（相互交流）
> - ③ 挂载后置定格：`between Chinese and foreign students`
> - ➔ **高质名词短语成品**：`mutual communication between Chinese and foreign students`

---

### 构件 2：高能动词短语 (`do sth`) 的行动承重分类库

句子要拒绝静态描述，多选用具有明确行为指向的高能动词：

1. **推进、激发与深化类**：
   - `cement enduring bonds of...`（加深/巩固持久纽带）；
   - `foster a supportive environment for...`（营造支持性氛围）；
   - `catalyze students' enthusiasm for...`（激发学生对……的热情）；
   - `enhance critical thinking and analytical competence`（提升批判性思维与分析素养）。
2. **化解、克服与整治类**：
   - `alleviate severe seat shortages`（缓解严重的座位紧缺）；
   - `curb the uncivilized practice of...`（杜绝……的不文明行为）；
   - `bridge the cultural communication gap`（消除文化沟通壁垒）；
   - `scrutinize sanitary vulnerabilities`（排查食品安全卫生隐患）。
3. **践行、落实与履职类**：
   - `serve as a keynote / guest speaker`（担任主旨/特邀嘉宾）；
   - `spearhead user-growth campaigns`（牵头推进用户增长项目）；
   - `prolong opening hours`（延长服务时间）；
   - `invoke the statutory refund policy`（依据法定政策主张退款）。

## 四、小作文五大高频场景重构字典（基础版与高级版对照）

### 场景 1：会议/活动主题与宗旨阐释
- **学生病句**：`The topic is Chinese culture. We hope students can learn something.`
- **基础版重构**（合规基础定从）：
  `This meeting focuses on traditional Chinese culture, which will help international peers understand our customs.`
- **高级版重构**（动宾承重 + 精准定格）：
  `Revolving around traditional Chinese culture, this session aims to cement enduring bonds of mutual understanding among international peers.`

### 场景 2：建议信举措提出
- **学生病句**：`The library should buy more books. It is helpful for our study.`
- **基础版重构**（明智之策 + 基础定从，**不用 thereby doing**）：
  `It would be a sound policy for the library administration to buy more academic books, which will help students prepare for exams.`
- **高级版重构**（考纲动宾承重 + 高阶名词化）：
  `It would be a sound policy for the administration to enrich the repository of authoritative academic reference volumes, which will notably facilitate students' in-depth research.`

### 场景 3：邀请信嘉宾角色期待
- **学生病句**：`We hope you come to our meeting because you know a lot of things.`
- **基础版重构**（得体同侪邀请）：
  `As an experienced student, you are warmly welcome to share your study methods with us.`
- **高级版重构**（同位语修饰 + 角色期待）：
  `Given your multicultural background, your presence as a guest speaker would provide fellow students with invaluable academic insights.`

### 场景 4：投诉信瑕疵与影响陈述
- **学生病句**：`The phone is bad. It cannot work. I feel angry.`
- **基础版重构**（事实陈述 + 逻辑因果）：
  `This newly purchased device keeps shutting down unexpectedly, which makes it impossible for me to study.`
- **高级版重构**（考纲动宾承重 + 瑕疵定格）：
  `This newly purchased gadget persistently suffers from erratic system freezes, which renders it virtually impossible for me to complete academic tasks.`

### 场景 5：回信建议与实操技巧
- **学生病句**：`You should practice your oral report. It is good for you.`
- **基础版重构**（建议结构 + 基础目的）：
  `It is advisable for you to practice your presentation with your friends, so that you can become confident.`
- **高级版重构**（动名词主语 + 动宾承重）：
  `Conducting a timed rehearsal in front of supportive peers will notably bolster your verbal fluency and onstage confidence.`
